What is Web3?

Web3 represents a fundamental shift from the current internet model to a user-owned, decentralized ecosystem.

User Ownership

Users own their data, identity, and digital assets instead of being the product of centralized platforms.

Trustless Security

Cryptographic principles ensure security without relying on centralized authorities or intermediaries.

Permissionless Innovation

Anyone can build, deploy, and participate without gatekeepers or approval processes.

Web2 (Current Internet)
Centralized data control by tech giants
Users are the product (data monetization)
Limited user ownership of digital assets
Web3 (Future Internet)
Decentralized ownership and control
Users own their data and identity
True digital ownership through NFTs

Core Web3 Technologies

The foundational building blocks powering the decentralized web

Decentralized Identity

Own your digital identity with self-sovereign credentials that can't be revoked or censored

Decentralized Storage

Store data on distributed networks like IPFS and Arweave with permanent, uncensorable access

Zero-Knowledge Proofs

Prove information without revealing the underlying data using advanced cryptography

DeFi & Tokenomics

Access decentralized financial services including lending, trading, and yield farming

Cross-Chain Interoperability

Seamlessly transfer assets and data between different blockchain networks

On-Chain Governance

Participate in decentralized decision-making through token-based voting systems
